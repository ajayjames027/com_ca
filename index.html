Competition Code

Rules & Regulations

Strict adherence to official guidelines is mandatory for all participating teams.

1. Team Formation

  • Each team must consist of exactly two members.
  • Students from UG Computer Science and Commerce Computer Applications are eligible to participate.
  • Teams may consist of students from the same department or a combination of students from both departments, subject to organizers' approval.
  • Each team must register with a unique team name.
  • Each participant can be a member of only one team.

2. Development Rules

  • Participants must develop their solutions within the time allotted by the organizers.
  • Teams must bring their own laptops and other required accessories.
  • Participants may use permitted programming languages, frameworks, libraries, and development tools.
  • The use of AI tools and code assistants must follow guidelines announced by the organizers.
  • Teams must ensure submitted work is 100% original and acknowledge all external libraries or datasets.
  • Plagiarism, copying, or submitting an existing project as newly developed is strictly prohibited.

3. Project Submission

  • Each team must submit its project before the deadline announced by the organizers.
  • The submission must include: Project Title, Problem Statement, Objectives, Proposed Solution, and Technologies Used.
  • Teams must provide the complete source code and a live working demonstration.
  • A brief presentation or PowerPoint slide deck may be required during the final evaluation.
  • Late submissions may be penalized or disqualified at the discretion of the organizers.

4. Discipline & Campus Entry

  • Mandatory: Participants must report on time and carry their original College ID cards.
  • Participants must carry their official Hackfest Entry Pass / ID Card downloaded from this portal for campus entry.
  • Teams must strictly follow the instructions of the organizing committee.
  • Misconduct or unfair practices will lead to immediate disqualification.
  • The judges' and organizers' decisions will be final and binding.

Academic Judging Standard

Evaluation Criteria

Projects will be critically evaluated by our jury of academicians and industry technologists based on six standard metrics.

Innovation & Creativity

Novelty of the concept, ingenuity in design, and unique approaches to real-world friction points.

Relevance to Problem

Alignment with the chosen problem statement and the depth of domain problem-solving.

Technical Implementation

Architecture, clean coding practices, technology stack mastery, and modular engineering.

Functionality & Live Demo

Flawless execution of working prototypes, UI responsiveness, and real data integration.

Social & Business Impact

Commercial viability, scalability, social usefulness, and potential for institutional adoption.

Presentation & Teamwork

Clarity in articulating solutions, effective Q&A defense, and balanced team collaboration.
